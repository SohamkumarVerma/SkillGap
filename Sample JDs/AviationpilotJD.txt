
# Job Description — Aviation Pilot

**Job Title:** Commercial Pilot
**Experience:** 0–5 years
**Location:** India / Domestic & International Operations
**Employment Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

We are looking for a skilled and safety-focused **Commercial Pilot** to operate passenger and cargo aircraft safely and efficiently. The ideal candidate should have strong knowledge of aviation procedures, aircraft systems, navigation, meteorology, and flight operations, along with excellent decision-making and communication skills.

### Key Responsibilities

* Operate aircraft safely in accordance with company procedures and aviation regulations.
* Conduct pre-flight, in-flight, and post-flight checks.
* Monitor aircraft systems, instruments, fuel levels, and flight parameters.
* Perform takeoffs, landings, climbs, descents, and other flight operations.
* Communicate effectively with Air Traffic Control (ATC) and flight crew.
* Analyze weather conditions and assess their impact on flight operations.
* Prepare and review flight plans, routes, fuel requirements, and alternates.
* Respond appropriately to abnormal and emergency situations.
* Maintain accurate flight logs and operational documentation.
* Follow Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), checklists, and Crew Resource Management (CRM) principles.
* Coordinate with cabin crew, dispatchers, ground personnel, and maintenance teams.
* Maintain awareness of aircraft performance, navigation, and operational limitations.

### Required Qualifications

* Valid **Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL)** or applicable aviation licence.
* Required aircraft-specific **type rating** for the assigned aircraft.
* Valid medical certificate as required by the applicable aviation authority.
* Strong understanding of aviation regulations and flight operations.
* Knowledge of aircraft systems, navigation, meteorology, and aerodynamics.
* Strong communication and decision-making skills.
* Ability to work effectively under pressure.
* Strong understanding of aviation safety procedures.

### Preferred Skills

* Experience with commercial airline operations.
* Instrument Rating (IR) and multi-engine experience.
* Familiarity with **IFR and VFR** operations.
* Knowledge of **ILS, VOR, GPS, and other navigation systems**.
* Understanding of aircraft performance calculations and fuel management.
* Strong Crew Resource Management (CRM) skills.
* Familiarity with Electronic Flight Bags (EFBs) and modern avionics.
* Knowledge of TCAS, GPWS/EGPWS, and other aircraft safety systems.
* Strong situational awareness and aeronautical decision-making skills.
* Experience with simulator-based training and recurrent checks.

### Key Skills / Keywords

**Commercial Pilot, CPL, Aviation, Flight Operations, Aircraft Systems, ATC, IFR, VFR, Navigation, Meteorology, Aerodynamics, CRM, Flight Planning, Aircraft Performance, Aviation Safety, ILS, GPS, Avionics, Emergency Procedures, Fuel Management**
