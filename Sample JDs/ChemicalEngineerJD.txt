# Job Description — Chemical Engineer

**Job Title:** Chemical Engineer
**Experience:** 0–3 years
**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra / On-site
**Employment Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

We are looking for a **Chemical Engineer** to support the design, optimization, and operation of chemical manufacturing processes. The ideal candidate should have a strong understanding of chemical engineering fundamentals, process safety, equipment design, and process optimization.

### Key Responsibilities

* Design, analyze, and optimize chemical manufacturing processes.
* Monitor production processes and identify opportunities to improve efficiency, quality, and yield.
* Perform **material and energy balances** for process operations.
* Analyze process data and troubleshoot operational problems.
* Assist in the design and selection of process equipment.
* Develop and review **Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs)** and Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs).
* Support process scale-up and implementation of new processes.
* Monitor key process parameters and ensure compliance with operating specifications.
* Conduct process safety assessments and participate in hazard analysis.
* Collaborate with production, maintenance, quality, and R&D teams.
* Prepare technical reports, process documentation, and operating procedures.
* Ensure processes comply with environmental, health, safety, and regulatory requirements.

### Required Qualifications

* Bachelor's degree in **Chemical Engineering** or a related field.
* Strong understanding of chemical engineering fundamentals.
* Knowledge of **thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, and mass transfer**.
* Understanding of chemical reaction engineering and process control.
* Ability to perform material and energy balances.
* Familiarity with process equipment such as reactors, heat exchangers, pumps, compressors, and distillation columns.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
* Good technical communication and documentation skills.

### Preferred Skills

* Experience with **Aspen Plus or Aspen HYSYS**.
* Knowledge of process simulation and process optimization.
* Familiarity with **PFDs and P&IDs**.
* Understanding of **HAZOP, HAZID, and process safety management**.
* Knowledge of instrumentation and process control systems.
* Familiarity with statistical process control and data analysis.
* Understanding of chemical plant utilities and energy management.
* Knowledge of environmental regulations and waste management.
* Experience with Excel, MATLAB, Python, or other engineering analysis tools.
* Familiarity with GMP/ISO standards is a plus.

### Key Skills / Keywords
