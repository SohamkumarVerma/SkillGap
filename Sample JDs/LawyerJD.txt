# Job Description — Corporate Lawyer

**Job Title:** Corporate Lawyer
**Experience:** 2–5 years
**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra / Hybrid
**Employment Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

We are looking for a skilled Corporate Lawyer to provide legal advice and support on corporate matters, contracts, regulatory compliance, and business transactions. The ideal candidate should have strong analytical, drafting, negotiation, and communication skills, with a good understanding of Indian corporate and commercial law.

### Key Responsibilities

* Draft, review, and negotiate commercial agreements, contracts, NDAs, and legal documents.
* Provide legal advice on corporate and commercial matters.
* Conduct legal research and interpret applicable laws and regulations.
* Ensure compliance with corporate and regulatory requirements.
* Assist with mergers, acquisitions, investments, and other business transactions.
* Identify and assess potential legal risks and recommend appropriate solutions.
* Manage contracts and maintain accurate legal documentation.
* Coordinate with external legal counsel when required.
* Support dispute resolution and litigation-related matters.
* Monitor changes in laws and regulations that may affect the organization.
* Communicate complex legal issues clearly to business and non-legal stakeholders.

### Required Qualifications

* LL.B. degree from a recognized institution.
* 2–5 years of experience in corporate/commercial law.
* Strong knowledge of Indian corporate and commercial laws.
* Excellent legal research and legal drafting skills.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills.
* Ability to analyze contracts and identify legal risks.
* Strong negotiation and problem-solving abilities.
* Good attention to detail.

### Preferred Skills

* Experience with **Companies Act, 2013** and related corporate regulations.
* Familiarity with **SEBI regulations** and corporate compliance.
* Knowledge of intellectual property and data-protection laws.
* Experience handling corporate transactions and due diligence.
* Familiarity with legal research databases and contract-management tools.
* Ability to work independently and manage multiple deadlines.

### Key Skills / Keywords

**Corporate Law, Contract Drafting, Legal Research, Contract Review, Compliance, Corporate Governance, Negotiation, Due Diligence, Risk Assessment, Companies Act, SEBI, Litigation, Intellectual Property, Regulatory Compliance**
