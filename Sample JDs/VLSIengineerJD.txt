# Job Description — VLSI Design Engineer

**Job Title:** VLSI Design Engineer
**Experience:** 0–3 years
**Location:** Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad / Hybrid
**Employment Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

We are looking for a **VLSI Design Engineer** to design, implement, and verify digital integrated circuits for high-performance semiconductor products. The ideal candidate should have a strong understanding of digital logic, computer architecture, RTL design, and hardware description languages.

### Key Responsibilities

* Design and develop digital circuits at the **RTL level**.
* Write synthesizable **Verilog/SystemVerilog** code.
* Develop and optimize RTL for performance, power, and area.
* Understand design specifications and translate them into hardware implementations.
* Perform functional simulation and debug RTL issues.
* Develop testbenches and work with verification teams to validate designs.
* Analyze synthesis results and timing reports.
* Assist with **static timing analysis (STA)** and timing closure.
* Identify and resolve design issues related to setup and hold timing.
* Collaborate with verification, physical design, and architecture teams.
* Follow RTL coding standards and maintain proper design documentation.
* Participate in design reviews and contribute to improving design quality.

### Required Qualifications

* Bachelor's degree in **Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering**, or a related field.
* Strong understanding of **digital logic and digital circuit design**.
* Knowledge of **VLSI design flow**.
* Proficiency in **Verilog or SystemVerilog**.
* Understanding of RTL design and synthesis.
* Knowledge of combinational and sequential logic.
* Understanding of flip-flops, registers, counters, multiplexers, and finite-state machines.
* Basic understanding of timing concepts such as **setup time, hold time, and clock skew**.
* Strong debugging and problem-solving skills.

### Preferred Skills

* Experience with **SystemVerilog, UVM, or RTL verification**.
* Knowledge of **ASIC and FPGA design flows**.
* Familiarity with synthesis and **static timing analysis** tools.
* Understanding of **floorplanning, placement, and routing**.
* Knowledge of **clock-tree synthesis (CTS)**.
* Familiarity with Design Rule Checking (DRC) and Layout Versus Schematic (LVS).
* Understanding of low-power design techniques.
* Knowledge of **CMOS technology and semiconductor fundamentals**.
* Experience with simulation tools such as QuestaSim, ModelSim, or equivalent.
* Familiarity with Linux, Git, and scripting languages such as Python or Tcl.

### Key Skills / Keywords

**VLSI, RTL Design, Verilog, SystemVerilog, Digital Logic, ASIC, FPGA, RTL Verification, Synthesis, Static Timing Analysis, STA, CMOS, Semiconductor, Physical Design, Timing Closure, Clock Tree Synthesis, UVM, DRC, LVS, Low Power Design**
